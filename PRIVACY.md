# Privacy Policy — ThumbsUp

_Last updated: 31 July 2026_

ThumbsUp does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

## What the extension does with your camera

ThumbsUp never requests camera access itself. When a video-call site you already gave camera
permission to starts your camera, ThumbsUp draws the reaction animations onto that video before the
site sends it, and looks at the frames to recognise hand gestures.

All of this happens **on your device, inside your browser**:

- Hand recognition uses a machine-learning model bundled with the extension. It is not a cloud
  service.
- Video frames, hand positions, and recognised gestures never leave the browser. Nothing is
  uploaded, logged, or saved to disk.
- The extension makes no network requests at runtime.

## What is stored

Your settings — whether ThumbsUp is on, which reactions are enabled, hold time, cooldown,
sensitivity, and any sites you disabled it on. These are kept in Chrome's own extension storage and
sync between your Chrome profiles if you have Chrome Sync turned on. They contain no personal
information.

## What is not collected

No analytics. No telemetry. No crash reporting. No accounts. No advertising identifiers. No sale or
sharing of data with anyone, because there is no data to share.

## Permissions and why they exist

- **Storage** — save your settings.
- **activeTab** — let the popup show whether ThumbsUp is running on the tab you are looking at, and
  play a test reaction when you press Test.
- **Site access** (Google Meet, Zoom, WhatsApp Web, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Discord, Whereby, Jitsi
  Meet, Slack, Around, Gather) — the extension can only work on a page where a call is happening. It
  runs on these video-call sites and nowhere else.
